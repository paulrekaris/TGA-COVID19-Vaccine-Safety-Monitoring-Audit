### Context

TGA officials testified regarding COVID-19 vaccine safety monitoring implementation during Supplementary Budget Estimates hearing.

### Key Exchange - Senator Antic & Dr Dascombe

**Senator Antic's Question:**
Asked whether the COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan (February 2021) was used, whether documents were collected, and if those documents are available.

**Dr Dascombe's Response (Key Points):**

1. **"Ample documentation" exists** demonstrating compliance with principles and objectives of the Plan

2. **"Five key elements... essentially describe our day-to-day processes"** - characterized Plan as describing routine post-market surveillance

3. **148 safety signals investigated** for COVID-19 vaccines since February 2021

4. **57 occasions of regulatory actions** taken by TGA

5. **"No single spot evaluation report"** outlining benchmarking against Plan elements - instead "ample information in public domain"

6. **"Vast volume of documents"** would relate to day-to-day work since February 2021

7. **Acknowledged difficulty**: "There would be some difficulty in doing that" (producing documents organized by Plan)

**Senator Antic's Follow-up:**
- Noted documents exist that aren't in public domain
- Proposed putting on notice request for those documents
- Stated: "These are just documents that show that adverse events reports were being done. This is a public document which in its essence was designed to give confidence to the Australian public that this pharmacovigilance was taking place."

**Critical Admission:**
> "The five key themes outlined in that report **essentially describe our day-to-day processes**. This is our standard sort of process in terms of post market surveillance and pharmacovigilance."

This confirms monitoring was routine operations, not a distinct enhanced framework tracked against Plan objectives.

### Significance for Audit

Senate testimony confirms findings from FOI requests and OAIC reviews that Plan implementation was not tracked as a distinct framework despite Cabinet endorsement and provisional approval requirements. The characterization of Plan strategies as "day-to-day processes" validates audit finding that enhanced monitoring was not implemented as operationally distinct from routine pharmacovigilance.
