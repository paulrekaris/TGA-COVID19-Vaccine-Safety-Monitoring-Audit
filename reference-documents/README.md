## Phillips 2021 – Vaccine Pharmacovigilance System

Phillips, A., Carlson, S., Danchin, M., Beard, F., & Macartney, K. (2021). From program suspension to the pandemic: A qualitative examination of Australia's vaccine pharmacovigilance system over 10 years. *Vaccine*, 39(40), 5968–5981.

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2021.07.059

Peer-reviewed analysis of Australia's vaccine pharmacovigilance system from 2009 to 2021. Provides independent assessment of systemic strengths and limitations in Australia's adverse event monitoring framework, contextualising the audit's findings regarding the gap between TGA's routine pharmacovigilance infrastructure and the enhanced monitoring commitments under the Safety Monitoring Plan.

**File:** [phillips-2021-vaccine-pharmacovigilance-system.pdf](phillips-2021-vaccine-pharmacovigilance-system.pdf)

## Sparke Helmore Legal Case Note

Independent legal analysis of OAIC Decision [2025] AICmr 54 ('AUQ' and Department of Health and Aged Care (Freedom of Information)).

Published by Sparke Helmore Lawyers, 11 May 2025.

Confirms the administrative law finding that TGA took "all reasonable steps" under s24A of the FOI Act and that the requested implementation documentation "cannot be found or does not exist." Provides independent legal validation of the OAIC decision cited throughout this audit.

**File:** [sparke-helmore-auq-dhac-foi-case-note.pdf](sparke-helmore-auq-dhac-foi-case-note.pdf)

## ICH E2E – Pharmacovigilance Planning

International Council for Harmonisation of Technical Requirements for Pharmaceuticals for Human Use (ICH). (2004). *ICH E2E: Pharmacovigilance Planning.* Step 4 Guideline.

https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/E2E_Guideline.pdf

International standard establishing requirements for pharmacovigilance planning as a condition of marketing authorisation, including signal detection, risk management, and periodic safety reporting. Applied in this audit as a benchmark for assessing whether TGA's Safety Monitoring Plan met international standards for post-market surveillance of provisionally approved medicines.

**File:** [e2e-guideline.pdf](e2e-guideline.pdf)

## CIOMS Working Group VIII – Signal Detection in Pharmacovigilance

Council for International Organizations of Medical Sciences (CIOMS). (2010). *Practical Aspects of Signal Detection in Pharmacovigilance: Report of CIOMS Working Group VIII.* Geneva: CIOMS.

https://cioms.ch/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/WG8-Signal-Detection.pdf

International guidance on signal detection methodology in pharmacovigilance, including quantitative methods, causality assessment, and regulatory response frameworks. Applied in this audit as a benchmark for assessing TGA's signal investigation and escalation processes against international best practice.

**File:** [wg8-signal-detection.pdf](wg8-signal-detection.pdf)
