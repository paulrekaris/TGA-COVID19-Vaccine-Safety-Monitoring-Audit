## Updates

### 22 October 2026: Analysis of Senate question on notice SQ25-003155 published

The Department's answer to SQ25-003155 (asked 12 December 2025) states that none of nine child deaths on the DAEN met the criteria to convene a VSIG, and that 15 VSIGs have reviewed over 50 cases since 2021, not all with a death outcome. The audit's analysis assesses these statements against the Plan's governance outputs. No ratings change. See [SQ13-19 gap analysis](analysis/tga-senate-qon-sq13-19-gap-analysis-2026.pdf)

### 16 October 2026: Version 1.9.6, 16 of 19 outputs partially documented or with no documentation located

[![Audit version 1.9.6](https://img.shields.io/static/v1?label=Version&message=v1.9.6&color=blue)](audit-governance/version-history.md)

- Three ratings under Strategy 2 (Enhanced signal detection) were revised from Not Documented to Partially Documented after review of FOI 5082 (pharmacovigilance system documents): outputs 2.2 (Enhanced individual-AEFI investigation), 2.3 (Enhanced cumulative data reviews) and 2.6 (Environmental scanning). Three outputs are fully documented, 13 partially and three have no documentation located. The overall finding remains NON-CONFORMING.
- New Federal Cabinet and state government sources record what governments committed to before the Plan, and provide a documentary benchmark for assessing its implementation. See the [Australian Government Policy folder](primary-sources/aust-gov-policy/README.md)

### 15 October 2026: FOI 27-3717, applicant proposes six signal groups

FOI request to the TGA for documents on AusVaxSafety COVID-19 vaccine safety signals. The TGA estimated 228 documents on 12 October 2026. The applicant proposed six signal groups in response. Consultation closes 26 October 2026. See [foi-27-3717](primary-sources/foi/foi-27-3717/README.md).

### 18 September 2026: Ombudsman refers complaint for further consideration

Complaint about the TGA's handling of FOI 25-0166 and FOI 3643. The Ombudsman referred complaint 2025-806374 for further consideration within the Office. See the [Ombudsman folder](primary-sources/ombudsman/README.md).

### 16 September 2026: FOI 27-3717 lodged

FOI request to the TGA for documents on AusVaxSafety COVID-19 vaccine safety signals. See [foi-27-3717](primary-sources/foi/foi-27-3717/README.md).

### 1 September 2026: Supporting material resubmitted to the Ombudsman

Complaint 2025-806374 about the TGA's handling of FOI 25-0166 and FOI 3643. The applicant resubmitted supporting material to the Ombudsman after the original attachments were recorded as corrupted.

### 6 July 2026: OAIC review awaiting allocation

Review of the TGA's refusal of FOI 25-0166, a request for documents on implementation of the Plan. The OAIC advised that review [MR25/01153](primary-sources/oaic/MR25-01153/README.md) awaits allocation to a review adviser.

### 1 June 2026: Manuscript submitted for peer review

A manuscript on regulatory compliance and documentary accountability, drawing on this audit, was submitted to a peer-reviewed journal and is under review.
