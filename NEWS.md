## 16 October 2026: Version 1.9.6, 16 of 19 outputs partially documented or with no documentation located

[![Audit version 1.9.6](https://img.shields.io/static/v1?label=Version&message=v1.9.6&color=blue)](audit-governance/version-history.md)

- Three outputs are fully documented, 13 partially and three have no documentation located. The overall finding is NON-CONFORMING. Three ratings were upgraded after review of FOI 5082.
- New Federal Cabinet and state government sources record what governments committed to before the Plan, and provide a documentary benchmark for assessing its implementation.

> [!NOTE]
> The audit assesses documentary evidence that the Plan's enhanced monitoring was implemented. It does not find that monitoring did not occur, only that implementation cannot be independently verified from the available record.

## 15 October 2026: FOI 27-3717, applicant proposes six signal groups

FOI request to the TGA for documents on AusVaxSafety vaccine safety signals. The TGA estimated 228 documents on 12 October 2026. The applicant proposed six signal groups in response. Consultation closes 26 October 2026. See [foi-27-3717](primary-sources/foi/foi-27-3717/README.md).

Review of the TGA's refusal of FOI 25-0166, a request for documents on implementation of the Plan. The OAIC advised that review [MR25/01153](primary-sources/oaic/MR25-01153/README.md) awaits allocation to a review adviser.
