Australia’s COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring: An Independent Audit

Did Australia’s drug regulator, the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), carry out the enhanced vaccine safety monitoring it promised? A four-year documentary audit of its compliance, tested against the plan’s own commitments.

When Australia approved COVID-19 vaccines for provisional use, its drug regulator, the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), published a February 2021 plan promising enhanced vaccine safety monitoring across the rollout. This is an independent audit and freedom-of-information investigation of whether that monitoring can be shown to have happened, assessing the TGA’s compliance against the plan’s own commitments using the international auditing standard ISO 19011:2018, Australian National Audit Office guidance, and open-source analysis of public records.

It examines 68.4 million doses (91.2% of Australia’s rollout) across four years. The question is narrow and verifiable: can the implementation of the vaccine safety monitoring plan, and the regulator’s compliance with it, be independently confirmed through records, as an enhanced framework relied upon for provisional approval requires?

The audit did not identify documentary evidence that the TGA systematically implemented the enhanced vaccine safety monitoring framework described in its February 2021 plan, leaving its compliance with that plan unverifiable from the documentary record. Of 19 audited plan outputs, only 3 are fully documented; the remaining 16 are partially documented or not documented at all.

What the audit found

  • 01In September 2024, directed by the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the TGA identified no implementation records for the safety monitoring plan — a position the Commissioner confirmed in Decision [2025] AICmr 54.
  • 02Senior TGA officials testified to the Senate (9 October 2025) that monitoring occurred through routine “day-to-day processes”, not as the distinct, systematically tracked framework the plan described.
  • 03The TGA reports 148 safety signals investigated and 57 regulatory actions taken, yet no documented audit trail links signals to actions or accounts for the disposition of the remaining 91.
  • 04The methodology is open, falsifiable and replicable. Every finding is traceable to a public or FOI-released source, and the full evidence base is published for independent scrutiny.

Questions

Did the TGA implement its COVID-19 vaccine safety monitoring plan?

On the documentary record, this cannot be confirmed. The audit found that 16 of 19 plan outputs are partially documented or not documented at all, and the regulator could produce no implementation records when directed to search for them.

Is this an official government audit?

No. It is an independent audit by a private researcher, conducted under ISO 19011:2018 using public and freedom-of-information records. It has been submitted to the Australian National Audit Office and cited in the Senate, but it is not a government report.

Can the findings be checked?

Yes. Every finding is traceable to a public or FOI-released source, and the full evidence base, methodology and dataset are openly published under a CC BY 4.0 licence for independent verification.
