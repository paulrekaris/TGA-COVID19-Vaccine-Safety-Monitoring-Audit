Australia’s COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring: An Independent Audit

Did Australia’s drug regulator, the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), carry out the enhanced vaccine safety monitoring it promised? A four-year documentary audit of its compliance, tested against the plan’s own commitments.

When Australia approved COVID-19 vaccines for provisional use, its drug regulator, the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA), published a February 2021 plan promising enhanced vaccine safety monitoring across the rollout. This is an independent audit and FOI-based investigation assessing whether implementation of that monitoring can be evidenced in the documentary record, using ISO 19011:2018, Australian National Audit Office guidance, and open-source public records.

It examines 68.4 million doses (91.2% of Australia’s rollout) across four years. The question is narrow and verifiable: can implementation of the vaccine safety monitoring plan, and regulatory compliance with it, be independently evidenced from the documentary record, as an enhanced framework associated with provisional approval requires?

The audit did not identify documentary evidence that the TGA systematically implemented the enhanced vaccine safety monitoring framework described in its February 2021 plan. As a result, implementation cannot be evidenced from the documentary record examined, leaving compliance not demonstrable from available records. Of 19 audited outputs, only 3 are fully documented; the remaining 16 are partially documented or not documented at all from the available material.

What the audit found

  • 01 In September 2024, directed by the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the TGA identified no implementation records for the safety monitoring plan — a position the Commissioner confirmed in Decision [2025] AICmr 54.
  • 02 Senior TGA officials testified to the Senate (9 October 2025) that monitoring occurred through routine “day-to-day processes”, not as the distinct, systematically tracked framework described in the plan.
  • 03 In its written response to Senate Question on Notice 559, the TGA reports 150 safety signals assessed and 57 regulatory actions undertaken. However, the response does not provide documented traceability between individual safety signal assessments and corresponding regulatory actions, nor does it describe the disposition of the remaining 93 signals within the scope of that response. The response provides no signal management documentation demonstrating alignment with the Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan’s 17 strategies and 2 governance outputs.
  • 04 The methodology is open, falsifiable and replicable. Every finding is traceable to a public or FOI-released source.

Questions and answers

Did the TGA implement its COVID-19 vaccine safety monitoring plan?

On the documentary record examined, implementation cannot be evidenced. The audit found that 16 of 19 outputs are partially documented or not documented.

Who conducted this research?

An independent researcher applying ISO 19011:2018 to public and FOI records.

Can the findings be checked?

Yes. All findings are traceable to published sources.

Can the audit be proven wrong?

Yes — any finding classified as not documented can be overturned by production of relevant records.
