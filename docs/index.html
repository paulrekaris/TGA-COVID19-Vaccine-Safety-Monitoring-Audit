Australia’s COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring: An Independent Audit

When Australia approved COVID-19 vaccines for provisional use, the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) published a February 2021 plan promising enhanced vaccine safety monitoring across the rollout. This is an independent audit assessing whether implementation can be evidenced in the documentary record using ISO 19011:2018 and FOI-based analysis.

The audit did not identify documentary evidence that the TGA systematically implemented the enhanced vaccine safety monitoring framework described in its February 2021 plan, leaving its compliance not demonstrable from the documentary record examined. Of 19 outputs, only 3 are fully documented.

What the audit found

  • 01 — No implementation records identified (OAIC confirmed).
  • 02 — Monitoring described as routine processes rather than structured framework.
  • 03 — QoN 559 shows 150 signals / 57 actions / no traceability for remaining 93.
  • 04 — Fully traceable, falsifiable methodology.

Questions and answers

Did the TGA implement its COVID-19 vaccine safety monitoring plan?

On the documentary record examined, implementation cannot be evidenced.

Who conducted this research?

An independent researcher applying ISO 19011:2018 to public records.

Can the findings be checked?

Yes. All findings are traceable to published sources.

Can the audit be proven wrong?

Yes — any finding classified as not documented can be overturned by relevant records.
