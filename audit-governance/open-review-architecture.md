# Open Review Architecture: Accountability Through Transparency

[![ISO 19011:2018](https://img.shields.io/badge/ISO-19011%3A2018-CC0000?labelColor=CC0000&style=flat-square)](https://www.iso.org/standard/70017.html) [![ISO 15489-1:2016](https://img.shields.io/badge/ISO-15489--1%3A2016-CC0000?labelColor=CC0000&style=flat-square)](https://www.iso.org/standard/62542.html) [![ANAO](https://img.shields.io/badge/ANAO-Audit%20Insights-1B2A4A?labelColor=1B2A4A&style=flat-square)](https://www.anao.gov.au/work/insights/performance-audit-process) [![FAIR Principles](https://img.shields.io/badge/FAIR-Data%20Principles-000000?labelColor=000000&style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18) [![Open Access](https://img.shields.io/badge/Open%20Access-MIT%20Press%202012-E67E22?labelColor=E67E22&style=flat-square)](https://cyber.harvard.edu/hoap/Open_Access_(the_book)) [![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Open%20Source-181717?labelColor=181717&style=flat-square)](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/exploring-projects-on-github/contributing-to-a-project) [![PGPA Act](https://img.shields.io/badge/PGPA%20Act-2013-1B2A4A?labelColor=1B2A4A&style=flat-square)](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2013A00123/latest/text)

This audit uses GitHub’s public, forkable and independently archived architecture as a primary accountability mechanism, exposing its evidence, methods, findings and revision history to continuous scrutiny. Unlike review confined to a closed process or single point in time, the evidentiary record remains available for anyone to verify, challenge, replicate or build upon. This open-review architecture complements formal scholarly and institutional review.

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the open review architecture underpinning this audit. It explains how the work enables continuing public accountability and scrutiny alongside formal scholarly and institutional review, and how the approach is informed by ISO 19011:2018 audit principles and relevant ANAO performance audit practice.

## 2. The Open Review Model

This audit implements a continuous, distributed, public review architecture:

| Feature                        | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public repository (GitHub)** | Full version control, transparent revision history, public by default                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Forkability (CC BY 4.0)**    | Anyone may copy, adapt, or redistribute. Forking is an invitation to correct, improve, or challenge                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Issues and discussion**      | Public forum for questions, critiques, and corrections                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Independent archiving**      | NLA (AWA), Harvard Dataverse, Zenodo, SSRN, SocArXiv, Mendeley Data (persistent identifiers where applicable), Arweave, and cryptographic timestamping. Multiple independent preservation and verification layers are designed to support long-term accessibility and make retrospective alteration detectable |
| **Deposited evidence**         | Audit-relevant FOI documents, evidence matrices, and primary sources preserved and citable                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Falsifiability**             | Findings are explicitly open to challenge and falsification through production of additional or contradictory documentary evidence; classifications and conclusions are revised where that evidence warrants it                                                                                                |

### 2.1. How to Engage

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Open%20Source-181717?labelColor=181717\&style=flat-square)](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/exploring-projects-on-github/contributing-to-a-project)

Any person or organisation may scrutinise, challenge, or build upon this work through:

* **Fork the repository** — create your own copy, correct errors, or publish alternative analysis
* **Open an Issue** — ask questions, raise concerns, or suggest corrections publicly
* **Cite the independent archives** — use persistent identifiers and archival records for scholarly or legal reference
* **Submit corrections** — email the author or open a pull request with proposed changes

Public engagement conducted through the repository may form part of its visible revision and discussion history, while independent archival preservation provides an additional record of the project.

## 3. Comparison to Traditional Peer Review

| Criterion          | Traditional Closed Peer Review                          | This Open Review Architecture                                                                                                   |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reviewers**      | Typically a small number of selected reviewers          | Open to unlimited public review                                                                                                 |
| **Timing**         | Review conducted at defined stages                      | Continuous and ongoing                                                                                                          |
| **Transparency**   | Reviewer comments generally not public                  | Public issues, forks, discussions                                                                                               |
| **Preservation**   | Primarily publisher-controlled                          | Multiple independent archives and cryptographic verification layers designed to support long-term preservation and verification |
| **Replicability**  | Underlying evidence and methods may not be fully shared | Audit evidence deposited and methods documented                                                                                 |
| **Accountability** | Formal reviewer and editorial processes                 | Public responses, corrections, challenges, or absence of response may be documented                                             |

## 4. Standards Alignment

**ISO 19011:2018** provides principles and guidance for evidence-based auditing, including the collection and verification of information and evaluation of audit evidence against audit criteria. This architecture supports those principles by preserving and citing the evidence base, documenting search methodologies and analytical decisions, and enabling independent examination and replication.

**ANAO performance audit practice** emphasises independent and objective assessment, defined audit objectives and criteria, evidence gathering and analysis, documented findings, and opportunities for audited entities to provide further documentary evidence and correct errors of fact. This architecture aligns with those practices by documenting its audit criteria and evidentiary basis, enabling scrutiny and correction, and maintaining publicly accessible records of the methodology, evidence and findings.

**ISO 15489-1:2016** establishes concepts and principles for the creation, capture and management of records, including characteristics of authoritative records such as authenticity, reliability, integrity and usability. The audit’s use of version control, cryptographic timestamping and multi-layer preservation is consistent with these records-management principles and supports the integrity and usability of its own evidentiary record. The repository also provides a publicly accessible revision history.

**Public Governance, Performance and Accountability Act 2013** (including ss 37–39), together with relevant provisions of the PGPA Rule, establishes Commonwealth entity obligations concerning performance records, measurement and assessment, and annual performance statements. These obligations apply at the accountable-entity level. The audit considers TGA monitoring activities within the Department of Health and Aged Care’s broader governance and performance framework.

## 5. Invitation to Formal Audit

This open architecture is not a substitute for formal audit by a statutory body with powers to obtain documents and evidence. It provides a publicly accessible and independently preserved evidentiary basis that may support such scrutiny.

The Australian National Audit Office (ANAO) is invited to consider a performance audit of the Department of Health and Aged Care’s governance of the TGA’s COVID-19 vaccine safety monitoring framework using its statutory powers. The Senate, OAIC, and Commonwealth Ombudsman are similarly invited to consider this work as a potential evidentiary basis for further inquiry or review.

## 6. Conclusion

This work does not claim that open review is equivalent to traditional peer review. It establishes a complementary accountability mechanism in which the evidence, methodology, findings and revision history remain exposed to continuing public scrutiny. Independent preservation ensures that the evidentiary record remains available for verification, challenge, replication and reuse over time.

* **Transparency** — audit evidence and methods are publicly accessible and independently archived
* **Verifiability** — anyone can examine, replicate, challenge, or build upon the work
* **Preservation** — multiple independent archival and cryptographic verification layers support long-term accessibility and make retrospective alteration detectable
* **Accountability** — the audited entity and oversight bodies are invited to respond; responses, corrections, challenges, or absence of response may be documented
