## Background
This complaint concerns the OAIC's administrative handling of IC review MR22/00538, which reviewed the TGA's refusal of FOI 3643 and ran from March 2022 until the decision of 26 March 2025 ([2025] AICmr 54). The Commonwealth Ombudsman was approached through the avenue identified in that decision. FOI 25-0166 and OAIC review MR25/01153 are subsequent context and are not grounds of the complaint.
