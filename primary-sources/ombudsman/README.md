# Commonwealth Ombudsman

## Case Details
- **Complaint reference:** 2025-806374
- **Date submitted:** 1 April 2025
- **Subject:** Contradictions in TGA responses and systemic issues in pharmacovigilance governance and FOI administration
- **Related FOI:** FOI 25-0166, FOI 3643
- **Related OAIC cases:** MR22/00538, MR25/01153

## Background
This complaint was submitted following documented contradictions between TGA's positions across multiple forums — FOI responses, Senate Estimates testimony, and OAIC proceedings. The complaint addresses both the specific handling of FOI 25-0166 and broader systemic concerns regarding TGA's pharmacovigilance governance and accountability framework.

The earlier FOI 3643 and OAIC review MR22/00538 established the initial pattern of TGA resistance to transparency, which escalated through FOI 25-0166 and MR25/01153.
