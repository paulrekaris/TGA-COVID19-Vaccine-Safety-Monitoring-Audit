### Context

TGA officials testified regarding COVID-19 vaccine safety monitoring implementation during Supplementary Budget Estimates hearing.

### Key Exchange - Senator Alex Antic & Dr Daniel Dascombe, Acting Medical Officer, Medicines Regulation Division, TGA

**Senator Antic's Question:**
Asked whether the COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan (February 2021) was used, whether documents were collected, and if those documents are available.

**Dr Dascombe's Response (Key Points):**

1. **"Ample documentation" exists** demonstrating compliance with principles and objectives of the Plan

2. **"Five key themes... essentially describe our day-to-day processes"** - characterised Plan as describing routine post-market surveillance

3. **148 safety signals investigated** for COVID-19 vaccines since February 2021

4. **57 occasions of regulatory actions** taken by TGA

5. **"No single spot evaluation report"** outlining benchmarking against Plan elements - instead "ample information in public domain"

6. **"Vast volume of documents"** would relate to day-to-day work since February 2021

7. **Acknowledged difficulty**: "There would be some difficulty in doing that" (producing documents organised by Plan)

**Dr Dascombe's Response (verbatim, Hansard page 32):**
> I would say that the TGA has ample documentation demonstrating compliance with the principles and objectives of that COVID vaccine safety monitoring plan. The five key elements that are articulated in that plan essentially describe the process of post market surveillance and safety investigation that our medicines and vaccine surveillance teams follow at the TGA every day. Whilst there is no single specific evaluation report outlining or benchmarking us against the elements of that plan, there is ample information in the public domain which provides evidence of compliance with the plan and underscores our commitment to transparency as a regulator. Since the time you mentioned, February 2021, and the start of the COVID vaccine rollout in Australia, the TGA has investigated 148 safety signals for COVID-19 vaccines. Updates have been made to product information and consumer medicine information documents on 57 occasions, representing regulatory actions taken by the TGA. The TGA's database of adverse event notification captures and reports on information reported by consumers, health professionals and jurisdictions in Australia. That's all publicly available. In addition, the TGA has released a number of documents under freedom of information. There have been 70 requests directed toward our branch since February 2021. To summarise, I would say that each of those elements is evidence of us working toward the principles and objectives contained in that.

**Senator Antic's Follow-up:**
- Noted documents exist that aren't in public domain
- Proposed putting on notice request for those documents
- Stated: "These are just documents that show that adverse events reports were being done. This is a public document which in its essence was designed to give confidence to the Australian public that this pharmacovigilance was taking place."

**Key Statement:**
> "The five key themes outlined in that report **essentially describe our day-to-day processes**. This is our standard sort of process in terms of post market surveillance and pharmacovigilance."

This is consistent with the audit finding that monitoring was not tracked as a distinct enhanced framework against Plan objectives.

### Significance for Audit

Senate testimony is consistent with findings from FOI requests and OAIC reviews that Plan implementation was not tracked as a distinct framework despite Cabinet endorsement and provisional approval requirements. The characterisation of Plan strategies as "day-to-day processes" is consistent with the audit finding that the documentary record does not show enhanced monitoring was implemented as operationally distinct from routine pharmacovigilance.
