## Notice of motion — 27 August 2025

On 27 August 2025, Senator Alex Antic gave notice of a motion seeking the production of documentation evidencing the Therapeutic Goods Administration's (TGA) adherence to and implementation of its *COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan*.

The requested documentation was to cover the period from February 2021 to the date of production, with production requested by **9.30 am on Monday, 29 September 2025**.

### Full text of the motion

> That there be laid on the table by the Minister representing the Minister for Health and Ageing, by no later than 9.30 am on Monday, 29 September 2025, all documentation created during the period from February 2021 to date evidencing the Therapeutic Goods Administration's (TGA) adherence to, and implementation of, the TGA's 'COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan' (the plan) including but not limited to:
>
> (a) documents evidencing the TGA's implementation of the plan's stated aims and key objectives being:
>
> (i) timely collection and management of reports of COVID-19 vaccine adverse events following immunisation,
>
> (ii) timely detection and investigation of COVID-19 vaccine safety signals,
>
> (iii) timely action to address any COVID-19 vaccine safety concerns,
>
> (iv) timely communications to inform the public of emerging COVID-19 vaccine safety information and to support public confidence in vaccines, and
>
> (v) close collaboration and coordination of effort with other vaccine safety stakeholder groups;
>
> (b) documents evidencing the TGA's implementation of the plan's five key strategies including the TGA's implementation of each of the strategies, objectives and outputs referenced in the key strategies of the plan as follows:
>
> (i) enhanced reporting of adverse events following immunisation,
>
> (ii) enhanced safety signal detection and investigation,
>
> (iii) actions in response to safety concerns,
>
> (iv) communications, and
>
> (v) collaborations; and
>
> (c) any revisions of the plan.

## Senate debate and Government response — 28 August 2025

The motion was considered by the Senate on 28 August 2025.

The motion was moved by **Senator Wendy Askew at the request of Senator Alex Antic**.

Senator Katy Gallagher, Minister for the Public Service, responded on behalf of the Government.

Gallagher stated:

> "The government does not support this motion from Senator Antic. It's another trawling expedition from an anti-vaccination senator."

She continued:

> "In this case, it seeks documents stretching back to February 2021. It's an unacceptable diversion of departmental resources."

Gallagher referred to the fact that the requested period covered the administrations of both the Morrison and Albanese governments.

She also stated:

> "Vaccination is the single most important step each of us can take to minimise the risk of severe disease and death associated with COVID-19 infection."

In relation to the TGA's safety-monitoring arrangements, Gallagher stated:

> "The TGA closely monitors the safety of COVID-19 vaccines and has well-established and robust systems in place to capture reports of suspected adverse events."

She further described the TGA's monitoring of vaccine safety and the continuing availability of safety information.

## Nature of the documentary request

The motion is notable for its specificity. It did not simply seek general information about COVID-19 vaccine safety. It sought **documentation evidencing the TGA's adherence to and implementation of its own Safety Monitoring Plan**.

The requested evidence covered:

### Plan objectives

- timely collection and management of adverse-event reports;
- timely detection and investigation of safety signals;
- timely action in response to safety concerns;
- timely communication of emerging safety information; and
- collaboration and coordination with vaccine-safety stakeholders.

### Five key strategies

- enhanced reporting of adverse events following immunisation;
- enhanced safety-signal detection and investigation;
- actions in response to safety concerns;
- communications; and
- collaborations.

### Other requested material

- documentation of implementation of the strategies, objectives and outputs; and
- any revisions of the Safety Monitoring Plan.

The requested period commenced in **February 2021**, corresponding to the commencement of the TGA's COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan.

## Relevance to this audit

The motion is significant to this audit because it independently identified the **documentary implementation of the TGA COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Plan** as a matter requiring parliamentary scrutiny.

The scope of the request closely corresponds to the implementation question examined by this audit: whether the Plan's stated objectives, strategies and outputs can subsequently be demonstrated through documentary evidence.

The parliamentary record therefore provides an important contemporaneous source showing that documentary evidence of implementation was itself the subject of formal parliamentary scrutiny in August 2025.

The motion does **not** establish the findings of this audit. It is preserved here as an independent parliamentary event relevant to the policy, regulatory and accountability context in which the documentary record was examined.

## Evidentiary significance

The August 2025 motion is preserved as a primary-source record because it provides contemporaneous evidence that Parliament sought documentation demonstrating implementation of the TGA's Safety Monitoring Plan.

The Government's response is also preserved because it provides the contemporaneous official position that the TGA had established systems for monitoring COVID-19 vaccine safety and capturing reports of suspected adverse events.

The audit independently examines the documentary record underlying implementation of the Plan. The parliamentary exchange is therefore treated as **context and provenance**, not as evidence validating the audit's findings.
