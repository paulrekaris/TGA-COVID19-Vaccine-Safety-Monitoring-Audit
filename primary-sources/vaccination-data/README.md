# Vaccination Data

This folder contains official COVID-19 vaccination administration data used in the audit to establish the scale of provisional approval deployment.

---

## COVID-19 Vaccination Data – 1 October 2025

Australian Immunisation Register (AIR) vaccination data as at 1 October 2025. Used to calculate total doses administered and the proportion administered under provisional approval.

**File:** [covid-19-vaccination-vaccination-data-1-october-2025.xlsx](covid-19-vaccination-vaccination-data-1-october-2025.xlsx)

## COVID-19 Vaccination Data – 27 July 2023

Australian Immunisation Register (AIR) vaccination data as at 27 July 2023. Used in the audit to establish that approximately 68.4 million doses were administered by 23 July 2023, representing approximately 91% of Australia's total COVID-19 vaccine rollout under provisional approval.

**File:** [covid-19-vaccination-vaccination-data-27-july-2023.xlsx](covid-19-vaccination-vaccination-data-27-july-2023.xlsx)
