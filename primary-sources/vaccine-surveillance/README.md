# Vaccine Surveillance

This folder contains vaccine surveillance reports and related materials used in the audit to assess TGA's reporting practices against Safety Monitoring Plan commitments.

---

## CDI 2024 – Surveillance of Adverse Events Following Immunisation in Australia (2021)

Communicable Diseases Intelligence (CDI) report on adverse events following immunisation (AEFI) in Australia for 2021, including COVID-19 vaccines. Used in the audit to contextualise TGA's AEFI reporting within the broader national surveillance framework.

**File:** [cdi-2024-surveillance-aefi-covid-19-2021.pdf](cdi-2024-surveillance-aefi-covid-19-2021.pdf)

## Department of Health Video – "No Different" (February 2021)

Transcript and documentation of Department of Health communications characterising COVID-19 vaccine monitoring as no different from standard vaccine safety monitoring processes. Contemporaneous evidence of how the government characterised the monitoring framework to the public during the provisional approval period.

**Evidentiary significance:** Provides context for assessing whether public communications accurately reflected the enhanced monitoring commitments made under the Safety Monitoring Plan.

**File:** [doh-video-no-different_feb2021.md](doh-video-no-different_feb2021.md)
