## FOI 27-3717 – AusVaxSafety COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Signals (September 2026, in progress)

Applicant lodges a request (16 September 2026) for documents from 1 February 2021 to 31 December 2023 concerning the TGA's receipt, assessment, investigation and regulatory consideration of COVID-19 vaccine safety signals derived from or informed by AusVaxSafety active surveillance, including the signals identified in the AusVaxSafety Annual Report 2022 released under [FOI 26-3110](https://www.tga.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/FOI%2026-3110.pdf).

TGA advised in its request consultation notice of 12 October 2026 that processing would be a substantial and unreasonable diversion of resources, estimating 228 documents, approximately 4,986 pages and more than 300 hours. The applicant responded on 15 October 2026 proposing a narrower scope (six signal groups from the AusVaxSafety Annual Report 2022). The consultation period closes on 26 October 2026.

**Evidentiary significance:** The request is unresolved, and no document has been released or examined. It relates to output 2.4 (active surveillance, AusVaxSafety), currently rated Partially documented, and to Finding 5 (AusVaxSafety signal pathways, a major non-conformity). The audit's current ratings do not depend on it. This entry records the request and consultation only. The folder and analysis will be updated after the conclusion of the FOI.
