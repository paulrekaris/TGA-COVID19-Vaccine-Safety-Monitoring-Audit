## Summary

Senator Roberts cited the TGA COVID-19 Vaccine Safety Monitoring Audit by name in the Australian Senate on 24 March 2026, referencing key findings from the SSRN publication.

## Key Messages Cited

1. The audit was conducted by Paul Rekaris using TGA's own data and documentation, based on four years of FOI requests
2. The audit is published on SSRN, the world's largest social science research network
3. The Commonwealth signed bilateral agreements with states requiring systematic vaccine safety monitoring via the TGA
4. National Cabinet endorsed the Australian COVID-19 Vaccination Policy in November 2020, operationalised through the TGA's February 2021 Safety Monitoring Plan
5. Only 3 of 19 Plan outputs are fully documented; 10 are partially documented; 6 have no documentation at all
6. TGA identified no implementation records when directed by the OAIC in September 2024 — confirmed by OAIC Decision [2025] AICmr 54
7. Monitoring was managed through routine day-to-day processes, contradicting the enhanced monitoring requirement for provisional approval
8. TGA investigated 148 safety signals and took 57 regulatory actions but published no documentation linking signals to actions
9. ISO 19011 conformity assessment reveals 0% full implementation for Objective 2 (Signal Detection) and Governance
